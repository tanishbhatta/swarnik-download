Install on Windows

  1. Download swarnik-pc.exe and open it. It installs by itself.
  2. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. The app is new and not signed yet.
  3. In Swarnik's Settings, Your PC, enter the PC's address and token, then Save and connect.
  4. Hold F9, speak, let go.

Private by design

No audio is saved. Your voice goes only to a Swarnik PC on a private network, never to an outside company. A better model on the PC reaches every laptop and phone at once.

Without access to a Swarnik PC, the app can't type anything.

Check the download (optional), in PowerShell: Get-FileHash swarnik-pc.exe
