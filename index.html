Install on Windows

  1. Download Swarnik.exe and open it.
  2. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. The app is new and not signed yet.
  3. Swarnik sets everything up by itself. Click Yes once when Windows asks to allow changes: that is Tailscale adding its network driver.
  4. When it says All set, hold F9, speak, let go.

Private by design

No audio is saved. Your voice goes only to a Swarnik PC on a private network, never to an outside company. A better model on the PC reaches every laptop and phone at once.

What the installer does: besides Swarnik, it installs Tailscale (downloaded from tailscale.com and checked for Tailscale's signature) and connects this computer to the private Swarnik network. On that network, your computer can reach only Swarnik's speech server, and nothing on your computer is opened to anyone. To leave: uninstall Tailscale and Swarnik in Windows Settings, Apps.

Swarnik needs the Swarnik PC to be on; when it is off, the app shows "PC not available" and types nothing.
